Developer: Cloe Ann Alde BSIT 3-C

Welcome to the ultimate mathematical adventure! Defeat monstrous foes, conquer endless waves in the Arena, and unlock legendary armors by solving fast-paced equations.

Developed specially for the MIT App Inventor Framework.

Quality Education

Math Hero supports UN Sustainable Development Goal 4 — ensuring inclusive, equitable quality education by making math learning engaging, accessible, and fun for all children through gamification.

Innovation

By blending RPG mechanics with curriculum-aligned math challenges, Math Hero drives educational innovation — transforming rote practice into an epic, skill-building journey that fosters critical thinking and a love for learning.
